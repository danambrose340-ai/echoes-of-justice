## About This Website

Echoes of Justice is a victim advocacy organization website serving the Colorado community. This site provides:

- **Case Archive**: Information about cases of public interest
- **Victim Stories**: Ways to share remembrance respectfully
- **Resources**: Directory of victim assistance and legal resources
- **Get Involved**: Opportunities to volunteer, donate, or partner
- **Contact**: Ways to reach our team

### Mission Statement

> "To stand with victims and families, preserve memory, promote transparency, and support positive change in communities touched by violence and injustice."

## Getting Started

### Navigation

Use the navigation menu at the top of every page:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **About** | Learn about our mission and values |
| **Cases** | Browse case archive |
| **Missing Persons** | Find help for missing persons cases |
| **Stories** | Read and share remembrance stories |
| **Justice** | Learn about legal resources |
| **Resources** | Access support organizations |
| **Get Involved** | Volunteer, donate, or partner |
| **Donate** | Support our mission financially |
| **Contact** | Reach out to our team |

### Mobile Experience

The site is fully responsive. On mobile devices:

1. Tap the hamburger menu (☰) to open navigation
2. Tap again or tap outside the menu to close
3. Forms and buttons are optimized for touch

## Site Features

### Case Archive

The Cases section displays case cards with:

- **Status indicator**: Color-coded status badges
- **Key information**: Name, date, location, summary
- **Case details**: Expanded facts and metadata
- **Submission link**: Submit new cases

### Victim Stories

The Stories section honors lives through:

- **Memorial cards**: Photos and remembrance quotes
- **Family statements**: Dignified sharing of memories
- **Consent-based**: Only published with explicit permission

### Resource Directory

The Resources section provides:

- **Categorized resources**: Victim assistance, legal, counseling
- **Contact information**: Phone numbers, websites, emails
- **Community organizations**: Local advocacy groups

### Donation System

The Donate section allows:

- **Suggested amounts**: Quick selection ($25, $50, $100, $250, $500)
- **Custom amounts**: Enter your own donation
- **Donation breakdown**: Where your money goes

### Forms

- **Contact Form**: General inquiries
- **Volunteer Application**: Get involved
- **Story Submission**: Share remembrance

## Accessibility

### Keyboard Navigation

All site features are accessible via keyboard:

- **Tab**: Move between interactive elements
- **Enter**: Activate links and buttons
- **Escape**: Close modals and dropdowns

### Screen Reader Support

The site includes:

- Proper heading hierarchy (H1-H3)
- Landmark regions (nav, main, footer)
- Image alt text for content images
- Form labels with aria attributes

### Customization

- **Text size**: Use browser's zoom (Cmd/Ctrl +)
- **Color contrast**: Meets WCAG AA standards
- **Reduced motion**: Respects system preferences

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Echoes of Justice a nonprofit?

No. Echoes of Justice is a mission-driven community advocacy project. It is not a licensed or registered nonprofit, and donations are not tax-deductible. Charitable status and a published accounting of funds are not yet in place.

### Can I submit information anonymously?

Yes. Submissions can include contact information only if you choose. All submissions are reviewed before any action.

### Will my story be published immediately?

No. Every story is reviewed, and nothing is published without your explicit consent.

### Does Echoes of Justice give legal advice?

No. We provide general information and advocacy support, not legal advice. For legal guidance, consult a licensed attorney.
